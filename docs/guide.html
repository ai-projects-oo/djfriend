DJ Guide

How to use DJFriend

Everything you need to go from an empty library to a polished, export-ready DJ set — step by step.

01

Getting started

DJFriend is a desktop app for Mac and Windows (and a web version at djfriend.onrender.com). Download the right build for your machine from the Downloads page and install it.

The main tabs are Set Generator, Library (your tracks and playlists), Crates (Discogs vinyl), History and Gig Finder. All analysis happens locally — your music never leaves your computer.

First time? Head to Settings (gear icon, top right) and add your Spotify Client ID and Secret. This enables genre data for every track. Without it the app still works — you'll just have fewer genre signals.
02

Load your library

Go to the Library tab and choose your source. DJFriend will scan, analyse, and cache the results in results.json in the same folder — subsequent loads are instant.

Local folder

Click Choose folder and point to any directory. Sub-folders are scanned recursively. Supported: MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV, M4A, OGG.

Apple Music

Mac only. Pick any playlist from your Apple Music library. DJFriend reads the local files directly — no export needed.

Rekordbox XML

Export your collection from Rekordbox (File → Export Collection as XML), then import the file. BPM and key tags carry over automatically.

M3U / TXT

Drop in any M3U, M3U8, or plain text file (one track per line). Works on the web version too, without audio files.

BPM doubling? If a track has an existing BPM tag, DJFriend reads it and uses it as a hint during analysis — this prevents the common ×2 detection error on slow tracks. If a track still shows a doubled BPM after loading, open the track editor and use the ÷2 button to halve it.
03

Playlists & folders

The Library tab has a sidebar, like Apple Music: All Tracks, your Playlists (with folders), and your sources — Apple Music imports, Spotify imports and saved sets.

1
Create

Click + next to Playlists (or 📁 for a folder) and type a name. Double-click any playlist or folder to rename it. Drag playlists onto folders to organise them.

2
Add tracks

Select tracks (click, ⌘-click, ⇧-click) and drag them onto a playlist in the sidebar. Or right-click → Add to Playlist ›, or Add to Last Playlist for the one you used last. Tracks already in the playlist are skipped — the notice tells you.

3
Order & tidy

Inside a playlist, drag rows to reorder (sorted by #). Sort by BPM, key or energy and click Save this order to keep it. Press Delete to remove selected tracks from the playlist (they stay in your library).

Show in Playlist

Right-click a track → Show in Playlist › lists every playlist it’s in (in their folders), plus Apple Music imports and saved sets. Pick one to jump there with the track selected. Selecting a track also marks its playlists in the sidebar with a dot, and the optional Playlists column shows how many playlists each track is in.

Made a mistake? Every playlist change can be undone — click Undo on the notice at the bottom, or press ⌘Z. Deleting a playlist or folder doesn’t ask first for this reason.
04

Smart playlists

Smart playlists fill themselves from rules and update as your library changes. Click ⚙ next to Playlists (or right-click a folder → New smart playlist here).

  • Rules — BPM, key (is or mixes with — harmonically compatible), energy, genre, vibe and mood tags, vocals, artist, title, comment, year, length, date added, playlist membership, times used in sets, and last time used in a set.
  • Match all / any — combine rules like Apple Music.
  • Limit — keep e.g. 50 tracks, chosen at random, by highest energy, newest, or least/most used in your sets.

The editor shows how many tracks match while you type. Use Edit Rules in the playlist header to change them, or right-click → Convert to Regular Playlist to freeze the current tracks.

Example: “Genre contains house” + “Key mixes with 8A” + “Last in a set not in the last 30 days” gives you fresh, compatible tracks for your next set.
05

Import a Spotify playlist

In the Library sidebar, under Spotify, click Import & match…. Paste a Spotify playlist URL (or the raw playlist ID) and click Import, or Browse my playlists. DJFriend finds the matching tracks in your local library and shows you what was found.

1
Copy the playlist URL

In Spotify: right-click the playlist → Share → Copy link to playlist. A full URL like https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9d… or just the 22-character ID both work.

2
Paste and import

Paste into the input field and click Import Playlist. Matched tracks are shown with a green checkmark; unmatched ones appear greyed out.

3
Use it in the generator

The playlist appears in the sidebar under Spotify. Open it and click Generate Set, or pick it in the generator’s Source card (see Generate from playlist). Right-click it → Save as playlist to make it a DJFriend playlist you can edit.

Tracks you don’t have? Click Create non-have on an import to get a private Spotify playlist of the missing tracks — a shopping list for your next record-store or Beatport session.
06

Energy curve

The energy curve defines the vibe arc of your set — how it rises, peaks, and falls. Draw it before generating.

Presets

Five presets cover the most common set shapes:

  • Warm-up — gentle ramp from low to mid energy
  • Peak-time — sustained high energy throughout
  • Festival arc — slow build to a sharp peak, then gradual decline
  • Closing — starts mid, peaks, then winds down
  • Roller-coaster — alternating highs and lows for variety

Drawing freehand

Click anywhere on the curve editor canvas to add a control point, then drag it. Right-click a point to remove it.

Tip: fewer control points = smoother curve = more musical transitions. Start with 3–4 points and add more only if you need a specific shape.
07

Preferences

The generator sidebar contains several controls that shape which tracks are eligible:

Venue & Set Phase

Choose your venue (Club, Bar, Wedding, Festival, Private event) and phase (Warm-up, Peak time, Cool-down, After-party). DJFriend will favour tracks that suit the vibe.

BPM Range

Set a minimum and maximum BPM. Only tracks within this range will be used.

Genre Filter

Genres are grouped into families (House, Techno, Disco & Indie, Breaks & Bass, Trance & Hard, Chill, Other), each with the number of tracks in your current source. Click a genre to add it, or a family name to add the whole family. Type in Find genre… to search. Your picks stay pinned at the top with their combined track count; genres too small to fill a set are dimmed. Leave it empty to use your full library.

Added Time Filter

Limit the set to recently added tracks: Last 30 days, 90 days, 120 days, This year, or All.

Set Duration

Choose a target length (30–180 min). The generated set will not exceed it.

Playlist mode auto-selects duration: when you switch to From Playlist source mode, the closest duration pill that fits the playlist is selected automatically and all shorter ones are disabled.
08

Generate from playlist

By default, DJFriend draws from your Full Library. The quickest way to build from a playlist is the Generate Set button in any playlist’s header in the Library (or right-click → Generate Set from Playlist) — it opens the generator with that playlist as the source and generates straight away. It works for your playlists, smart playlists, Apple Music and Spotify imports.

You can also choose the source yourself in the Source card in the generator sidebar:

1
Toggle to "From Playlist"

Click the From Playlist button in the Source card. A dropdown lists your playlists (♫), smart playlists, and Apple Music and Spotify imports. You can tick several.

2
Pick a playlist

Select the playlist you want to work from. The duration pill auto-selects to the shortest option that fits the playlist's total length. Shorter pills are disabled.

3
Generate

Hit Generate. The set is built from that playlist only. Your other preferences (genre, BPM, curve) still apply.

This is the ideal workflow for a booked set: import the playlist you've prepared in Spotify, then let DJFriend find the best harmonic order within it.
09

Generate the set

Press the ▶ Generate button. DJFriend builds the full set automatically based on your curve, preferences, and library.

Get a different result

Click ▶ Generate again — each run produces a slightly different set. Use Generate from remaining to replace only the tracks after a specific position while keeping the ones before it.

10

Fit indicators

Every track in the generated set shows a small coloured dot in the # column. Hover over it to see exactly why a track might not fit.

Green — good fit. No issues with this track.

Amber — minor issue. Hover to see what's slightly off — could be energy, BPM, or genre.

Red — needs replacing. Hover to see the problem — harmonic clash, energy mismatch, or a big BPM jump.

The stats bar at the top shows how many tracks have issues. Click the count to jump to the first one.

11

Swapping tracks

Every track row has a ⟳ Swap button. Click it to open the smart swap panel.

DJFriend looks through your full library and shows up to 5 replacement tracks, sorted best to worst fit. If nothing suitable is found it says so. Click any suggestion to swap it in.

Manual reordering

Drag any track row to a new position using the grip handle on the left. The fit indicators update immediately to reflect the new order.

Removing a track

Click the ✕ Remove button on any row to delete it from the set. The track remains in your library — it is simply not part of this set.

12

Editing track metadata

Click the ✎ Edit button on any row to open the inline editor. You can update:

  • Title & Artist — change the display name.
  • BPM — manual override. Use ×2 / ÷2 to fix a halved or doubled reading in one click.
  • Key / Camelot — correct the key if it doesn't match what your DJ software shows.
  • Genre — add or change the genre.

Changes apply immediately and are saved for future sessions.

13

Export

Click the ↓ Export button at the top right of the set list to open the export menu.

M3U playlist

Exports an M3U file with absolute file paths. Open it directly in Rekordbox, Serato, VirtualDJ, Traktor, or any other DJ software that reads M3U. The file preserves track order and includes the runtime of each track.

Save as Playlist

Saves the set as a DJFriend playlist in the Library, so you can edit it, generate from it again, or push it to Apple Music.

Rekordbox XML

Downloads a Rekordbox XML file (or Send to Rekordbox when a Rekordbox folder is set). Vinyl-only records are left out; the M3U export lists them as # VINYL: lines so you know where to drop the record.

Spotify playlist

Sends the set to your Spotify account as a new private playlist. Requires Spotify credentials in Settings. Tracks that can't be found on Spotify are skipped.

Pro tip: use the Spotify export after a gig to save the actual set you played — import the playlist, generate from it in source mode, and you have a reproducible template for future bookings.
14

Apple Music export

Mac only. Your playlists live in DJFriend; push them to Apple Music when you want them there (and on your iPhone).

  • Push one playlist — right-click it → Push to Apple Music.
  • Push a folder — right-click a folder → Push folder to Apple Music.
  • Export everything — click ⇪ on the sidebar’s Apple Music header.

Everything goes into a DJFriend folder in Music, keeping your folders. Pushing again updates the same playlists (renamed, re-ordered, tracks added or removed) — it never duplicates them, and never touches your other Music playlists. Linked playlists show a small in the sidebar.

Options

  • Add tracks that aren’t in Music yet — imports the local file into your Music library first (on by default).
  • Write BPM and key — writes DJFriend’s BPM and Camelot key onto the Music tracks — key in the Grouping field, or in front of the Comment — so Rekordbox and Serato see them.
The first export asks you to let DJFriend control Music — click OK. If you declined, enable it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.
15

Player & queue

Double-click any track to play it — playback continues through the rest of the list. Use ▶ Play or ⤮ Shuffle in a playlist header to play the whole playlist.

The player bar stays at the bottom of every tab: previous / play / next, waveform seeking, shuffle, repeat (all or one), and volume. Open the queue (♫ icon) to see what’s next, drag to reorder or remove tracks.

  • Play Next / Play Later — right-click tracks to queue them right after the current track or at the end.
  • Keyboard — Space plays/pauses; your Mac’s media keys work too.
16

Settings

Open Settings via the gear icon in the top-right corner. The main sections:

Spotify credentials

Enter your Spotify Client ID and Client Secret to enable genre data and Spotify export. Get them free at developer.spotify.com/dashboard — create an app and copy the credentials.

OpenAI (optional)

Paste an OpenAI API key (create one at platform.openai.com/api-keys) to turn on the optional AI features, such as BPM and key estimates for vinyl records. The key is stored only on your device; OpenAI bills per request. Vibe, mood and venue tags are computed locally and work without a key.

Library path

Shows the currently loaded folder. Change it here to switch between libraries.
